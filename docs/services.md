# Services — Mohammed Taha EL AHMAR

## Working with him

Mohammed Taha works with two kinds of people: companies hiring for senior or lead data engineering roles, and teams looking for a freelance data engineer for a defined mission. Both can start with the assistant on this site, the Services page, or a 30-minute call.

## Freelance offer

**Architecture and cost review**
A focused review of an existing data platform: architecture, pipelines, data quality, security and cloud costs. The outcome is a written assessment with prioritised, concrete recommendations the team can act on.

**Delivery mission**
Hands-on design and delivery of data platforms and pipelines on Databricks, Azure, AWS or GCP: ingestion, transformation, orchestration, data quality and governance, through to production with CI/CD and monitoring. He can lead the technical work or join an existing team.

**Training and coaching**
Trainings and workshops for data teams, drawn from the trainings he already delivers: Databricks, generative AI for data teams, and data quality. Sessions can be in English or French.

## How he works

He favours simple, maintainable designs over fashionable ones, writes things down (architecture decisions, runbooks), leaves the team able to run what was built, and is explicit about trade-offs and costs.

## Rates, dates and contract terms

Daily rates, start dates, duration and contract terms depend on the mission and are discussed on a call. Use the "Book a call" button or email med.taha.elahmar@gmail.com.

## Checking fit for a project

Clients can paste a project brief into the assistant (the "Paste a job description or brief" option) to get an evidence-based view of how his experience matches the mission, with sources from his resume and projects.
