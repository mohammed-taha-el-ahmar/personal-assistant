summary: Researchers evaluated 'narrative deception' strategies where LLM-driven honeypots generate coherent, mutually referential artefacts to counter autonomous LLM attackers. In a campaign of 1,150 simulated SSH sessions, coherent scenarios increased median session length for advanced attackers from 40 to 165 commands and reduced abandonment rates from 86% to 52% compared to static or less sophisticated conditions.
