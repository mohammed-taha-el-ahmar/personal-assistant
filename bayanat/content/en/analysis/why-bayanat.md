Data and AI teams in Europe, the Middle East and Africa are asked to do three things at once: ship useful AI, keep their data trustworthy, and stay ahead of rules that keep moving. Most coverage treats these as separate worlds. Bayanat treats them as one job.

## What we cover

- **Governance and master data**: catalogs, lineage, data quality, data contracts, reference data.
- **Enterprise LLMs**, seen from the data side: governed retrieval, model governance, evaluation, cost.
- **Data and AI security**: leaks, access control, prompt injection, vulnerabilities in the tools you run.
- **Regulation** as a common thread, with a [tracker](../../tracker/) of the dates that matter.

## How we use AI

A pipeline collects official pages, release notes, advisories and research, sorts them and drafts short summaries. The editor reads every item, checks it against its source, adds a point of view and decides what goes out. Nothing is published automatically. Our [editorial charter](../../charter/) sets the rules, including on sponsors.

## What you get

One email every Tuesday, readable in eight minutes, and an analysis every two weeks. Free, and easy to leave.
